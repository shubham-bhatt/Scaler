# Structural Design Patterns

> Patterns for *composing objects and classes into larger structures* while keeping
> them flexible. They answer "how do these pieces fit together?" without rigid
> inheritance.

**Topics:** Adapter · Decorator · Facade · Composite · Proxy

---

## Adapter

_Not yet written._ (Convert one interface into another the client expects; wrap a
legacy/third-party class; object vs class adapter.)

---

## Decorator

_Not yet written._ (Add responsibilities dynamically by wrapping; vs subclassing;
java.io streams as the canonical example.)

---

## Facade

_Not yet written._ (A simple unified interface over a complex subsystem; reduces
coupling between client and internals.)

---

## Composite

_Not yet written._ (Treat individual objects and compositions uniformly via a tree;
file-system / UI-component hierarchies.)

---

## Proxy

_Not yet written._ (A stand-in controlling access: virtual (lazy), protection,
remote, caching proxies.)
